# CustomUI Studio Privacy Policy

CustomUI Studio does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

The application runs entirely on your computer. It does not connect to the Internet, contains no telemetry, advertising, or analytics tools, and does not create any user accounts.

The Excel workbooks, XML files, images, and VBA modules that you open or create remain on your computer, in locations of your choosing. They are never sent to the author or to any third party.

The application only stores the following on your computer:

- the chosen interface language;
- a cache of icons provided by your Microsoft Excel installation, to allow for faster display.

This data never leaves your computer and is deleted along with the application.

Contact: customuistudio@gmail.com
