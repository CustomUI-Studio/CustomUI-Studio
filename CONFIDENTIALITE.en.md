# CustomUI Studio Privacy Policy

CustomUI Studio does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

The application runs entirely on your computer. It does not connect to the Internet, contains no telemetry, advertising, or analytics tools, and does not create any user accounts.

The Excel workbooks, XML files, images, and VBA modules that you open or create remain on your computer, in locations of your choosing. They are never sent to the author or to any third party.

## AI Assistant

By default, the AI Assistant connects to nothing: you copy the request into the AI tool of your choice yourself, then paste its answer into the application.

If you turn on the "AI on this computer" mode, the application sends the request shown on screen to an AI that you installed yourself on your computer (for example Ollama or LM Studio). Only addresses of your own computer are accepted: nothing is sent over the Internet or to your local network. The request contains the description of what you want and, if you leave it on, the ribbon structure (IDs, labels, procedure names); never your VBA code, your images, or your file names. The request and the answer are not saved.

The application only stores the following on your computer:

- the chosen interface language;
- a cache of icons provided by your Microsoft Excel installation, to allow for faster display;
- the location of the component library, if you choose one;
- the settings of the "AI on this computer" mode, if you use it: on or off, server type, address, model, and time limit.

This data never leaves your computer and is deleted along with the application.
